# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

Transportia+ ("App") is a fork of
[Transportia](https://github.com/Wafler1/transportia) by Wafler.one, maintained
by [blauertee](https://github.com/blauertee). This policy explains what
information is (and is not) collected, and applies to every version of the App
that links to it.

## 1. Data We Do Not Collect

The App has no server of its own. It does not collect, store, or sell personal
data, and runs no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Local Device Storage

Your settings, saved places and saved trips are stored only on your device,
until you delete the App or clear its data.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App calls these services directly; they receive your IP address and what
each request needs:

- the routing server, by default [Transitous](https://transitous.org):
  searches, start and end points, times, and the map area shown;
- [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org): the map area shown;
- OpenStreetMap's [Nominatim](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org): a place you
  tap on the map (can be turned off under Location).

We do not control how they process data. Please review their privacy policies.

## 4. Location

If you allow location access, your position is used on your device, and sent
to the routing server only when you plan a trip from or to "My Location".

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

Updates are posted in this file; its history is public.

## 7. Contact

Questions? Use the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/blauertee/transportia-plus/issues) or the
contact under About Transportia+ in the App.
